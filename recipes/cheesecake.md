# Cheesecake

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 200 g digestive biscuits
- 100 g melted butter

### Filling

- 600 g cream cheese
- 150 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 100 ml whipping cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C.
2. Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs.
3. Mix the crumbs with the melted butter.
4. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a springform pan.
5. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
6. Add the eggs one at a time and mix gently.
7. Stir in the whipping cream, vanilla extract, and cornstarch.
8. Pour the filling over the crust.
9. Bake for 50–60 minutes, until the centre is slightly wobbly.
10. Let the cheesecake cool, then refrigerate it for at least four hours.

## Serving suggestion

Serve chilled with fresh berries or jam.
